## 1. What data do we collect?

**Account details**
- Your email address — to sign you in, and to send verification and password
  reset codes.
- Your password — stored only in a form that cannot be reversed (a hash); we
  cannot see it either.
- If you use the app without an account, no email is kept; only an anonymous
  ID is created.

**Profile details**
- Your display name.
- Your gender (female, male or "prefer not to say") — to show it on your
  player card. If you choose "prefer not to say", it is shown nowhere.
- Your username (e.g. @makikali) — so your rivals can find you. It is made
  from your display name; you can change it in Settings.
- Your profile photo — **optional** (details in section 4).
- Your language (Turkish or English) — so notifications and emails reach you
  in your language.

**Content you enter**
- Your match records: rival names, set scores, date, time, court name and
  your match notes.
- Your rival links and your match proposals (day, time, place).
- Tournaments: tournament name, tournament photo (optional), participants,
  pairings, match times and scores.

**Notifications**
- If you allow notifications, your device's notification address (push
  token) and your notification preference.

**Usage data** (you can turn it off in Settings → Usage data)
- Which screens you open and what you do in the app (for example logging a
  match, starting a tournament, sharing an invite); the app version, device
  model and operating system.
- It is linked only to your account's random ID. Your name, email, rivals and
  scores are never sent.

**Safety records**
- Photo reports you make and that are made about you, and the people you
  block.
- Photo moderation results (accepted/rejected and scores — not the photo
  itself).

**What we don't collect:** your location, your contacts, your photo library
as a whole, your advertising ID. KortPro has no advertising, we don't track
you across other apps and websites, and your data is not sold.

## 2. Who can see your information?

There are no public profiles. A KortPro user with an account can search for
you by typing at least the first 3 letters of your username or of a word in
your display name. Search results list your display name and username (at most
20 people per search), and they can send you a link request. Your profile
photo appears in search results only if you allowed it (Settings → Preferences
→ "Others besides my rivals may see my photo"; off by default). Your stats are
shown only if you accept the request.

You can turn off appearing in search in Settings → Preferences → "Others can
find me by name and username"; then nobody can find you and your invite link
does not work, but you can still add rivals yourself. People you have blocked,
or who have blocked you, can never find you.

Visibility works like this:

| Information | Only you | Your linked rivals | Players in the same tournament |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your email address | ✓ | | |
| Your match notes | ✓ | | |
| Rival names you typed, your personal matches | ✓ | | |
| Your display name, profile photo, gender (if you stated it) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Your username | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Your match count and win rate — only from matches against linked rivals and in tournaments | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| A match you recorded with a linked rival (score, date, time, court — not the note) | ✓ | ✓ (that rival only) | |
| Match proposals | ✓ | ✓ (only the other side of the proposal) | |
| Your tournament results, match times, tournament photo | ✓ | | ✓ |

People you block cannot see your profile photo, and cannot send you link
requests or match proposals.

**Share cards:** You can share a match, your stats or your score against a
rival as a picture. The card shows your name and photo, your rival's name and
the scores. Your rival's photo appears only if they allowed it (Settings →
Preferences → "Others besides my rivals may see my photo"; off by default — the
same consent covers the photo in search results). A picture you share can be seen by
others wherever you share it.

## 3. Why do we process it? (Legal grounds)

- **To provide the service** — creating your account, keeping your matches,
  connecting you with rivals, running tournaments, sending notifications.
  KVKK Art. 5/2-c (establishing and performing a contract).
- **Safety and preventing abuse** — automatic photo moderation, reports,
  blocking. KVKK Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest).
- **Improving the app** — seeing which features are used (usage data). KVKK
  Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest). You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Legal obligations** — removing unlawful content and requests from public
  authorities. KVKK Art. 5/2-ç.

Your data is not used for advertising, profiling or facial recognition.

## 4. Profile and tournament photos

- Uploading a photo is optional. Your photo library is not accessed; the app
  only sees the photo you choose.
- On your phone, the photo is resized to 512×512 pixels and saved again. In
  the process, the information embedded in it (where it was taken, the
  device, the date) is **removed**.
- Every photo is checked **automatically** for inappropriate content (nudity,
  violence and so on) before it goes live. For this, the photo is sent to the
  content moderation service of our provider OpenAI. A photo that fails the
  check is deleted immediately.
- Photos are kept in a private store and shown only to the people listed in
  section 2, through links that are valid for a short time.
- An inappropriate photo can be reported from the app; a reported photo is
  hidden until it is reviewed.
- When you remove or change your photo, or delete your account, it is deleted
  from the server too.

## 5. Service providers and transfers abroad

| Provider | What for | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, file storage, sign-in | Germany (Frankfurt) |
| Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) | Relaying notifications | USA |
| Apple (APNs), Google (FCM) | Delivering notifications to your device | USA |
| OpenAI, L.L.C. | Only the automatic moderation of uploaded photos | USA |
| PostHog, Inc. | Usage data (can be turned off) | Germany (Frankfurt) |

OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models; it may
keep it for up to 30 days to monitor abuse.

Because these providers' servers are outside Türkiye, using KortPro means
your data is transferred abroad within the meaning of KVKK Art. 9.

We keep these transfers limited:

- **Only the data that is needed is sent.** Each provider receives only what
  it needs for the job listed in the table. OpenAI receives only the photo
  you upload — not your name, your email or your matches. PostHog receives
  only the usage data described in section 1.
- **Your database is in the European Union** (Germany), in a data centre
  subject to EU data protection rules.

For questions and requests about transfers abroad, write to
support@kortpro.app.

## 6. How long do we keep it?

- Your account and content: until you delete your account.
- Photos: until you remove or change them, or delete your account.
- Records of sent notifications: 30 days.
- Photo moderation records: separated from your identity when your account is
  deleted.

## 7. Deleting your account

Settings → **Delete account**. Your account, profile, photos, matches,
rivals, links and proposals are deleted permanently.

- Matches you shared with linked rivals are deleted from their lists too.
- Matches played in tournaments remain for the other players; "Unknown" is
  shown in place of your name.

## 8. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is
processed, to request information, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted,
to learn who it has been transferred to, to object, and to claim
compensation for damages. Write to **support@kortpro.app** with your
requests; we reply within 30 days at the latest.

## 9. Security

Connections are encrypted (TLS). In the database every record is protected by
row-level access rules: you cannot read or change anyone else's data. Photos
are kept in a private store.

## 10. Children

KortPro is not designed for children under 13. If you are under 18, use the
app with your parent's or guardian's knowledge and consent.
