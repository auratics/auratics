# Fast Tracker Support

If you have a problem, a question or an idea, write to
**auratics.studio@gmail.com**.

It helps if you include your phone model and the version of the app.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Fast Tracker works without an account, and your fasts stay on your phone.
An account is only for Backup and sync.

**I started fasting earlier. Can I set the start time?**
Yes. Before you tap **Start fasting**, choose how many hours ago you started,
or tap **Specify time** to pick the exact time.

**How do I fix a fast I already ended?**
Open History and tap the fast. You can change its start, its end and its
goal, then tap **Save**.

**I forgot to log a fast. Can I add it?**
Yes. On the Timer screen, tap **Add fasting record**.

**What do the colours in the calendar mean?**
Days with a fast of 12 hours or more are green. The darker the green, the
longer the fast: 12, 14, 16, 18 and 24 hours.

**Will my fasts move to a new phone?**
Yes, with Backup and sync. Turn it on in Settings on your current phone, sign
in with Google or Apple, then sign in the same way on the new phone. Without
it, your fasts are stored only on the phone you use now, and uninstalling the
app deletes them.

**Why are there ads?**
Ads keep Fast Tracker free. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you can change
whether ads are personalised in the app's settings.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Fast Tracker is a timer and a log. Talk to a doctor before fasting,
especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes or another
medical condition, take medication, or have had an eating disorder.
