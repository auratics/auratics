About me

I started my career in ice cream in 2015, and when I opened my small shop in 2020 I decided to work with my philosophy: not using the industry and producing everything I could, from dried fruit pastes to my bean to bar chocolate, selecting farmers and plantations directly, without intermediaries.

Read more

I always say: less is more, that is, in my micro ice cream shop you will find very few flavours, all seasonal, created every morning, an ice cream free of emulsifiers, thickeners and preservatives. Every day at 5 in the morning I get up to make it fresh, a few kilos every day, and when a flavour runs out it is not repeated the same day.

I don't have any type of storage and I'm really small, the shop measures only 27 m². Here you can find excellence and research in every handmade and refined process to maximize the true flavour of every ingredient!

What's inside, and what's not

What's in my gelato

A real ice cream, made every morning, always fresh, without being frozen or stored. A real clean, authentic, ethical gelato!

We only use

  • Fresh organic milk from our hills, Az. Agr. Biologica Bordona
  • Organic eggs from our lands in Sasso Marconi, Az. Agr. Rainbow Cocche
  • Whole organic fruit, never pureed or semi-finished products
  • High-quality organic chocolate that we make ourselves in our lab from cocoa beans (lecithin-free)
  • Whole sustainable nuts
  • Organic raw honey from our hills, Az. Agr. Podere 101, or even from my own apiary
  • Cane or white sugar

We never use

  • Thickeners and gums (carob or other)
  • Milk powder
  • Dry glucose
  • Corn syrup
  • Maltodextrin
  • Inulin
  • Colorants
  • Dextrose

Our philosophy

Real gelato
is art

Making ice cream, for us, does not mean following fixed formulas. It's listening to nature, understanding its changes and finding a new balance every day.

01It lives only one day, once exposed to the air.

02It cannot be put in the freezer at home: the deep cold ruins the texture and the soul.

03Ice cream should be eaten fresh, freshly made. Just like nature thought.

Not only gelato, but also craft chocolate

The love for real chocolate began 10 years ago. I really start from the selection of the farmer and the plantation, which must respect very strict standards, and I carry out every phase in my laboratory.

Cleaning and manual selection of the cocoa beans
Roasting, which seeks the maximum expression of the cocoa bean
Refined in a stone machine for 72 hours, adding only cane sugar or white beet sugar
At least four months vacuum-packed, to let the natural aromas mature

Every bite awakens new emotions and flavours, a limited edition chocolate in every bar.

Where quality coffee meets true artisan gelato

Specialty affogato

In my gelato shop, affogato is not just a dessert. It is an encounter.

There isn't just one affogato. There is a meeting between a specific gelato and a precise coffee. My role is to create balance. A properly extracted espresso should not overpower the gelato, but open it.

Specialty affogato is rarely found in traditional gelato shops. Here, it is not an addition to the menu. It is a project.
