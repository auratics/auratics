About me

I always say: less is more, that is, in my micro ice cream shop you will find very few flavors, all seasonal, created every morning, an ice cream free of emulsifiers, thickeners and preservatives, every day at 5 in the morning I get up to make it fresh, a few kilos every day and when a flavor runs out it is not repeated the same day, I don't have any type of storage and I'm really small, the shop measures only 27 m2, here you can find excellence and research in every handmade and refined process to maximize the true flavor of every ingredient!

What we use in the lab

What's in my gelato

A real ice cream, made every morning, always fresh, without being frozen or stored, a real clean, authentic, ethical GELATO!

We only use

  • Fresh organic milk from our hills Az.agr Biologica Bordona
  • Organic eggs (from our lands in Sasso Marconi) Az.agr Rainbow cocche
  • Whole organic fruit (never pureed or semi-finished products)
  • High-quality organic chocolate that we make ourselves in our lab from cocoa beans (lecithin-free)
  • Whole sustainable nuts
  • Organic raw honey from our hills Az.agr Podere 101 or even from my own apiary
  • Cane or white sugar

We never use

  • Thickeners and gums (carob or other)
  • Milk powder
  • Glucose dry
  • Corn syrup
  • Maltodextrin
  • Inulin
  • Colorants
  • Dextrose

What's inside my GELATO ... or it would be better to say what's not inside my GELATO , inside my ice cream there are no chemical additives of any kind, there are no thickeners, there are no chemical emulsifiers, there are no stabilizers, there is no powdered milk and there is not even condensed milk, there is no dry glucose, there are no maltodextrins or inulin, there are not the classic ingredients that you find in 99.9% of Italian and world ice creams.

Well now I can tell you what's in my ice cream, my ice cream is made starting from raw milk from a local company that is analyzed every week to ensure the highest quality of proteins, fats and fibers, I only use high quality fresh cream, classic white sugar or cane sugar, honey, inverted sugar that I create naturally in my laboratory, fresh eggs from local farms (rainbow cocche), salt from different origins in the world, organic fresh fruit sorbets favoring slow food ingredients!

Making ice cream

Real gelato
is art

Making ice cream, for us, does not mean following fixed formulas. It's listening to nature, understanding its changes and finding a new balance every day.

We only use live, non-industrial ingredients. This means that every day brings something different: the milk changes with the seasons and with the grass that the cows eat, the fruit varies in its sugar and water, even two strawberries from the same plant are never the same. We constantly add the recipes, to favour these changes and transform them into perfect taste, structure and consistency.

For this, our ice cream:

01It lives only one day, once exposed to the air.

02It cannot be put in the freezer at home: the deep cold ruins the texture and the soul.

03Ice cream should be eaten fresh, freshly made. Just like nature thought.

Our ice cream is born every morning in our open kitchen, always prepared fresh! Only real ingredients, no preservatives.

With few ingredients, a lot of technique, and absolute respect for the raw material, we treat ice cream as a living craft, made of precision, instinct and passion.

Our Philosophy:

Free gelato, without compromise.

Respect for nature, from the earth to the cone.

A flavor that speaks truth, different every day.

The love for real chocolate began 10 years ago now, true because I really start from the selection of the farmer and the plantation, which must respect very strict standards, I carry out every phase in my laboratory, from cleaning and manual selection, to roasting which seeks the maximum expression of the cocoa bean which can recall red fruits, or even yellow ones like bananas, each cocoa bean has a different flavour, after which the peel is separated from the inside, the peel is used for infusions and also for my personal garden, the cocoa beans are refined in a stone machine for 72 hours by adding only cane sugar or white beet sugar, after which it is vacuum-packed for at least four months to allow all the natural aromas of the beans to mature of cocoa and after which it is used to create my chocolate sorbet in the ice cream shop or in the months that allow it I manually produce every single bean to bar bar

Where quality coffee meets true artisan gelato

Specialty affogato

In my gelato shop, affogato is not just a dessert. It is an encounter.

It comes from the idea that true artisan gelato — made with living ingredients, without industrial shortcuts — can speak with a coffee extracted with the same care, the same obsession for detail, the same respect for origin. This is why I call it a specialty affogato.

Read more

I use carefully selected, traceable coffees, handled with attention at every stage: from the plantation to roasting, all the way to extraction. Every origin has its own voice — floral, citrusy, chocolatey, spicy. There isn’t just one affogato. There is a meeting between a specific gelato and a precise coffee. My role is to create balance. A properly extracted espresso should not overpower the gelato, but open it. A vanilla cream should not sweeten the coffee, but support it. Even a sorbet can create unexpected contrasts — fresh, vibrant, alive. For me, specialty is not a trend. It is a method. It is respect for raw ingredients. It is transparency. It is study. I bring into my gelato shop the same approach I apply to every flavor: conscious origin selection, clean processing, and the pursuit of natural balance. Specialty affogato is rarely found in traditional gelato shops. Here, it is not an addition to the menu. It is a project. It is the point where the world of quality coffee meets true artisan gelato. And this is only the beginning.
