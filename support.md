## Frequently asked

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Open the app, go to Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently
removes your account and all associated data.

**Do I need an account?**
No. You can use the app as a guest. Creating an account only adds syncing
across devices.

**I found a bug.**
Please email us with your device model and iOS version and we will look
into it.
