# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy applies to all mobile applications published by Auratics Studio.

## Data we collect

**Account data.** Some of our apps let you create an account. Where they do, we store your email address to authenticate you. Some apps also let you use them as a guest, in which case an anonymous identifier is created instead and no email is stored. Apps that do not offer accounts collect no account data at all.

**Content you create.** The information you enter in an app is stored so it can be shown back to you and, where the app supports accounts, synced to your devices. This content is linked to your account or anonymous identifier and is not visible to other users. Apps without accounts keep this information on your device only.

**Advertising data.** We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device information and advertising identifiers to serve ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we show non-personalized ads on iOS.

## How data is stored

Where an app stores data on our servers, it is held on Supabase. Access is restricted by row-level security, so only your own account can read your records. Where an app stores data only on your device, that data never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Deleting your data

In apps that offer accounts, you can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account). This permanently removes your account and all associated content from our servers.

In apps that store data only on your device, uninstalling the app removes all of it.
