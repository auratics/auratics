# Sugar Free Streak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy applies to the **Sugar Free Streak: Quit Sugar** app for Android
and iOS, named Sugar Free on the home screen and published in the app stores
by **auratics**.

**Data controller:** Feyza Can\
**Contact:** auratics.studio@gmail.com

**In short**
- You can start without signing up. The app gives you a guest account that
  holds no name and no email.
- What you log stays on your phone unless you turn on **Back up my logs**. With
  it on, a copy is kept with your account on our server in the European Union.
- There are no ads and no analytics, and we do not sell your data.

---

## 1. What stays on your phone

- What you log: each time you tap **I had sugar** or **I beat a craving**,
  with the day and time.
- Whether you agreed to backup, and your settings, such as the theme and the
  daily reminder.

Uninstalling the app deletes this from the phone.

## 2. Your account

When you first open the app, it creates a guest account for you with
Supabase, our backend provider. The guest account is an account ID and the
time it was created; it has no name and no email.

You can turn it into a full account by adding an email address and a password,
so you can sign in on another phone. Then we also store that email address and
a hash of the password (never the password itself), and Supabase emails you a
code to confirm the address.

## 3. Back up my logs (optional)

The app asks, before anything you log leaves the phone, whether you want your
logs backed up. "Keep them on this phone only" is a choice of equal weight, and
you can change your answer in Settings at any time.

**What we store with backup on, on Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany:** for each
log, whether it was sugar or a craving you beat, the calendar day, the time,
and when it reached our server.

**What for:** only to keep a copy of your logs and to show the same streak on
every phone you sign in on. Every account can read only its own logs.

**Turning backup off** stops new logs from being sent. Logs already backed up
stay with your account until you delete them (section 9).

## 4. Your health

What you eat can say something about your health, which the GDPR treats as a
special category of data. Without backup your logs never leave your phone.
With it, we store them only because you explicitly agreed, and we never share
them with anyone else.

Sugar Free Streak is a habit tool. It gives no medical advice and is not a
medical device. If you have diabetes, an eating disorder or another medical
condition, talk to a doctor about changes to your diet.

## 5. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone. Nothing is
sent to us or to another server to deliver it.

## 6. Services the app connects to

| Provider | Purpose | What it receives | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase, Inc. | Your account; backup if you turn it on; checking whether a newer version of the app is needed | As described in sections 2 and 3 | Germany (Frankfurt) |
| Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) | Downloading app updates | Device type, app version and a random installation ID | United States |
| Apple or Google | Installing the app and showing notifications | As described in their own policies | United States |

Like any request over the internet, each of these also reaches the provider
with your IP address. None of them receives your logs except Supabase, and
only with backup on.

## 7. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

- **Backup of your logs:** your explicit consent (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and
  9(2)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by turning backup off and deleting
  your data.
- **Your account and update checks:** what is needed to provide the app you
  asked for (Article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in keeping it
  working and secure (Article 6(1)(f)).

## 8. How long data is kept

- On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Your account and its logs on our server: until you delete them.
- Expo, Apple and Google keep the data they receive according to their own
  policies.

## 9. Deleting your data

- **One log:** tap **Undo** right after logging it.
- **Your account and everything stored with it:** Settings → **Delete
  account** (for a guest, **Delete my data**). This deletes the account and
  every log on our server and on the phone, right away.
- **Without the app:** write to **auratics.studio@gmail.com** from the email
  address of your account, and we delete it within 30 days.

Step by step: [Deleting your data](delete-account)

## 10. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct and
delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data
portability, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to
your local data protection authority.

For a copy of what we store about your account, or any other request, write
to **auratics.studio@gmail.com** and we will reply within 30 days.

## 11. Security

Your logs are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, which other
apps cannot read. Every connection the app makes is encrypted (TLS). On our
server, row level security limits every account to its own data.

## 12. If you used Portföy Takip

Sugar Free Streak replaces auratics' earlier Portföy Takip app. The portfolio
records of its users are kept, unchanged and unused, for about 30 days after
Sugar Free Streak's release so that copies of the old app keep working, and are
deleted by 31 December 2026 at the latest. To get a copy
before then, or to have them deleted sooner, write to
**auratics.studio@gmail.com**.

## 13. Children

Sugar Free Streak is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in
countries where that is the age of digital consent. We do not knowingly
collect data from children.

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with
the date at the top.
