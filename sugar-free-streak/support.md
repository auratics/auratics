# Sugar Free Streak Support

If you have a problem, a question or an idea, write to
**support@auratics.app**. It helps if you include your phone model and
the app's version, which is at the bottom of Settings.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. The app starts with a guest account. Add an email in Settings if you want
to keep your streak on another phone.

**How is my streak counted?**
It is the number of days since you last logged sugar. A day with sugar starts
it again from 0; your best streak stays.

**I tapped "I had sugar" by mistake.**
Tap **Undo** in the message that appears right after.

**Does beating a craving change my streak?**
No. It is counted on its own, so you can see how many you got through.

**Will my streak move to a new phone?**
Yes, if **Back up my logs** is on and you have added an email. Sign in with the
same email on the new phone. Without backup, your logs are only on the phone
you use now, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. What you log is saved on the phone and sent when you are back online.

**Is this medical advice?**
No. Sugar Free Streak is a habit tool. If you have diabetes, an eating disorder
or another medical condition, talk to a doctor.
