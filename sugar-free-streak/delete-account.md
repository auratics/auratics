# Sugar Free Streak: deleting your account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and data in **Sugar Free Streak:
Quit Sugar** (Sugar Free on the home screen), an app by **auratics**.

## In the app

1. Open Sugar Free Streak and tap the gear at the top right.
2. At the bottom of Settings, tap **Delete account**. As a guest, it is called
   **Delete my data**.
3. Confirm twice.

Your account and every log stored with it are deleted from our server and
from the phone right away. A guest then starts again with a new, empty guest
account.

## Without the app

Email **support@auratics.app** from your account's email address, with the
subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and data, and confirm by
email within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account: its ID, your email address and your password hash.
- Every log backed up with it: sugar and beaten cravings, with their days and
  times.
- If you used Portföy Takip with the same account, its portfolio records too.

## What is kept

Nothing. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
