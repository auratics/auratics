Account & Data Deletion Request

To delete your account and all associated data from our app, you can use either of the methods below.

Method 1 — From within the app

Open the app → go to Settings → tap Delete Account. Your account and data will be deleted immediately.

Method 2 — From this page (if you no longer have the app)

Fill out the form below and submit it. Your request will be processed within 30 days and all data associated with your account will be permanently deleted.

When your account is deleted, your profile information, in-app data, and all personal data associated with your account will be permanently removed. Certain data may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, in accordance with applicable law.
