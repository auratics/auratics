Account & Data Deletion Request

To delete your account and all associated data, open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account.

If you no longer have access to the app, send us an email from your registered address and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Email us: auratics.studio@gmail.com

When your account is deleted, your profile information and all personal data associated with your account will be permanently removed. Some data may be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.
