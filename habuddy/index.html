Snap it

Every check‑in is a photo.

Take a photo when you do the thing: a run, a stretch, a page of your book. Add a caption if you like. Your crew gets it as a notification, photo and all.

Every photo is checked for inappropriate content before anyone else sees it.

See the week

One grid, one row each.

Everyone in the habit has a row. Each day is a square that fills with that day's photo, so you see at a glance who showed up and who still has to.

Free shares a habit with one buddy. Premium brings the whole crew and every past week.
