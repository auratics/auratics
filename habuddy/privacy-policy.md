# Habuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy applies to the **Habuddy** app, previously published as **Habitual**. The app is published on the App Store and Google Play under the developer name **auratics**.

**Data controller:** Feyza Can, trading as auratics\
**Contact:** auratics.studio@gmail.com

Habuddy is a habit tracker you can use alone or with friends. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your email address, and your sign-in with Apple or Google if you use one. If you sign in with email, your password is stored in hashed form. Older versions of the app could also be used as a guest, with an anonymous ID and no email.
- **Your profile:** the name you choose and your profile photo, if you add one.
- **Your habits:** their names, notes, icons, schedules and reminder times, and each day you check in.
- **Check-in photos:** any photo you add to a check-in. Location and other metadata are removed on your phone before it is sent.
- **Friends and shared habits:** who you are friends with, the habits you share, cheers, nudges, and anyone you have blocked.
- **Reports:** if you report a photo or a person, or someone reports yours, the report and its outcome.
- **Notifications:** a token that lets us send notifications to your phone.
- **Purchases:** whether you have Premium, and which plan. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never see your card.
- **Usage data:** which screens you open and which features you use, with your account ID, to improve the app. You can turn this off in Settings → Share usage data.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps.

## Who can see what

Your email is never shown to other people. Friends see your name and profile photo, and so does anyone you send an invite to, before they accept it. People in a habit you share also see that habit's check-ins and photos. Everything else is visible only to you. Your photos are private files, reachable only by the people above.

## Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** stores your account and data and runs our servers.
- **OpenAI** checks each uploaded photo for content our rules do not allow. The photo is sent to its moderation service; it is not used to identify you.
- **RevenueCat** tracks the state of your subscription.
- **Expo, Apple and Google** deliver notifications.
- **Apple and Google** handle sign-in with them, and purchases.
- **PostHog** receives usage data, unless you turn it off.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. On a free account, check-in photos are deleted 30 days after the check-in; the check-in itself stays. Premium keeps them for as long as you have Premium. A photo you remove, or a habit you delete, is deleted for good, and removing yourself from a shared habit deletes your check-ins and photos in it.

## Your choices

You can change or remove your name and photo in the app, and turn off usage data and streak alerts in Settings. You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account, which permanently removes your data; [Delete your account](delete-account) has the steps, including what to do without the app. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the address below.

## Children

Habuddy is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app.
