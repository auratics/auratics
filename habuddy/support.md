# Habuddy support

Have a problem, a question or an idea? Write to **support@auratics.app**. Including your phone model and the app version (Me → Settings) helps us answer faster.

## Common questions

**Do my friends see all my habits?**
No. Friends see your name and photo. Only the people in a habit you share see that habit's check-ins and photos.

**How do I cancel Premium?**
In your App Store or Google Play account's subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.

**A reminder didn't arrive.**
Check that notifications are on for Habuddy in your phone's settings. Reminders are set on your phone, and open the app once in a while to keep them coming.

**How do I report someone?**
Tap their name in the Friends tab or in a shared habit. To report a photo, open it and tap the flag.

**How do I delete my account?**
See [Delete your account](delete-account).
