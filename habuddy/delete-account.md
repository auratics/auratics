# Habuddy: delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and data in the **Habuddy** app, previously published as **Habitual**, developed by **auratics**.

## In the app

1. Open Habuddy and go to the **Me** tab.
2. Tap the settings icon at the top.
3. At the very bottom, tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and permanently. If you signed in with Apple, you are asked to confirm with Apple once more, so that the app's access is revoked too.

If you are using an older version as a guest, you have no account to delete: delete your habits in the app and uninstall it, or write to us as below.

## Without the app

Email **support@auratics.app** from your account's email address, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete your account and data, and confirm by email within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account: email address, sign-in and name.
- Your profile photo and check-in photos.
- Your habits, check-ins and reminders.
- Your friendships, your memberships in shared habits and your check-ins in them. A habit you own that others share is deleted for them too.
- Reports made by you or about you.

## What is kept

- Purchase records that Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep for tax and accounting. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.

Deleted data cannot be recovered.
